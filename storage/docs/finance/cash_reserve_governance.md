# TechMart Working Capital & Cash Reserve Governance

## 1. Statutory Operating Cash Buffer
- Minimum required untouchable treasury reserve: **₹5,00,000** (Strictly reserved for payroll, tax, and warehouse facility leases).
- Unallocated Free Cash Flow available for procurement: Current Total Cash minus Statutory Buffer.

## 2. Procurement Allocation Ceilings
- For any single emergency replenishment event, the maximum authorized upfront cash outflow must not exceed **40% of unallocated free cash flow** (Max ₹1,80,000 for standard replenishment cycles unless executive override is granted).
- Trade credit terms (Net-30 / Net-60) must be utilized preferentially when upfront cash is restricted.
