# Master Supplier Contracts & Credit Terms

## Vendor Reference Matrix (Confidential - Finance Access Only)

### 1. Supplier A — Apex Global Logistics (Vendor ID: SUP-A)
- **Base Pricing**: 1.00x Base Catalog Cost (P100 = ₹450/unit).
- **Payment Terms**: **100% Advance Payment on Purchase Order generation**.
- **Lead Time**: 12 to 14 Business Days.
- **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)**: 300 units.
- **Order Capacity Limit**: 1,500 units per month.
- **Default / Cancellation Policy**: Non-refundable 15% deposit once production begins.

### 2. Supplier B — QuickLogix Domestic Express (Vendor ID: SUP-B)
- **Base Pricing**: +6% Premium over Base Catalog Cost (P100 = ₹477/unit).
- **Payment Terms**: **Net-30 Days invoice settlement** (0% cash upfront required).
- **Credit Limit**: Active credit line of ₹5,00,000.
- **Lead Time**: Guaranteed **3 Business Days** via domestic express air freight.
- **Order Capacity Limit**: Max 400 units per single order; max 800 units/month.

### 3. Supplier C — Zenith Direct Components (Vendor ID: SUP-C)
- **Base Pricing**: -3% Discount on Base Catalog Cost (P100 = ₹436.50/unit).
- **Payment Terms**: **50% Advance Wire Transfer upon PO / 50% upon Warehouse Dock delivery**.
- **Lead Time**: Standard **7 Business Days**.
- **Order Capacity Limit**: Max 600 units per order.
- **Special Contract Clause 4.2 (Surge Relief)**:
  * In confirmed emergency stockout situations, Supplier C allows a surge capacity increase of +15% (up to 690 units) with an accelerated 5-day delivery for a flat ₹8/unit expedite surcharge.
