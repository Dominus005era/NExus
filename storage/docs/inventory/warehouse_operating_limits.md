# TechMart Warehouse Operating Limits & Inbound Capacity

## 1. Physical Capacity & Staging Constraints
- **Main Warehouse (Bhiwandi Hub)**: Total capacity 25,000 volumetric units. Current utilization: 68%.
- **Dock Inbound Staging Limits**:
  - Maximum inbound pallets per day: 12 pallets (~2,500 units max throughput).
  - Unloading & Quality Inspection turnaround time: 24 hours from freight arrival.

## 2. Inbound Staging Priority Rules
- In the event of critical stockouts (< 2 days buffer), inbound shipments tagged "EMERGENCY_RUSH" bypass standard staging and enter direct fulfillment queue.
